name=MUT Model Builder
qgisMinimumVersion=3.28
description=Define a regional hydrogeological model, build surfaces and meshes for MUT, and write a starter _build.mut.
about=A wizard collects the project name, description, location, model boundary (square, rectangle drawn on the map, watershed found online by name, or vector file), existing-data folder and output folder. The plugin then uses local rasters where available, otherwise downloads a regional ~30 m DEM (NRCan MRDEM-30 bare-earth DTM in Canada, Copernicus GLO-30 elsewhere) and a bedrock surface (local file, or the global Shangguan et al. 2017 depth to bedrock). "New focus area..." outlines named subregions of the regional model (rectangle, digitized outline, vector file, or a feature such as a wetland plus its local watershed delineated with GRASS or Global Watersheds); after the user accepts the extent it builds a ~1 m ground surface from public lidar (USGS 3DEP, NRCan HRDEM, AHN, UK Environment Agency). Lakes, which lidar shows as a flat water surface, are lowered to a lake bottom from local bathymetry files, Ontario MNRF surveys, or an estimate from lake shape and surrounding slope (clearly flagged as estimated); dams on those lakes are reported with measured reservoir levels (GRCA) and published target elevations. The regional run also retrieves domain-mean precipitation and temperature (Daymet or Open-Meteo) and outlet river flows (ECCC or USGS). "New mesh..." builds a 2D Delaunay triangle mesh. "Add streams..." (separate menu) finds rivers on a built mesh and either attaches them as CLN (Streams_CLN.xyzList; MUT cln mesh intersection) or rebuilds with fixed stream nodes and .nchos files. "Build MUT project..." writes 2_models/<name>/_build.mut with Surficial (10 log-proportional sublayers) over Bedrock (5 log-proportional sublayers to a flat base 200 m below the lowest surficial base; GWF materials 6 and 21), a default long-term average P−ET climate forcing as a 3000 d transient spin-up (net P−ET to CLN then SWF, level-1 critical-depth outlet; Abdul-style ATS) or optional monthly transient P/EVT, plus elevation list files. Outputs are GeoTIFFs in a UTM projection, a QGIS project and Markdown reports.
